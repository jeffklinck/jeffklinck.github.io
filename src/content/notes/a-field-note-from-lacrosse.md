Good defence looks reactive from the stands. Up close, most of the work happens earlier: recognizing a shape, communicating the next rotation, and moving before the obvious threat develops.

Short, repeated calls create a shared model of the field. Film and agreed rules reduce the choices that must be invented under pressure. The same principle applies to technical systems: clear messages and explicit responsibilities make coordinated action easier.
