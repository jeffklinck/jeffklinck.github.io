title: Reading around money and institutions
date: "2026-09-18"
summary: A short reading list on payment rails, settlement, and the institutional choices hidden inside financial infrastructure.
---

Payment systems are often described as neutral plumbing. They are not. A rail decides who may participate, which risks are mutualized, how quickly claims become final, and what kinds of institutions can sit at the edge of the network.

Three questions are especially useful: what the settlement asset is, where finality occurs, and who may build at the network’s edge. Technical architecture and institutional design are inseparable; each determines which risks and powers the system distributes.
