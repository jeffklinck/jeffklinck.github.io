## About

I study [Engineering Physics](https://engsci.utoronto.ca/program/majors/engineering-physics/) at the University of Toronto. I have taken a broad range of courses spanning modern & classical physics, computer science & AI, and electrical engineering.

I am grateful to have been working in [Professor Andreas Veneris’s](https://www.ece.utoronto.ca/people/veneris-a/) blockchain-focused research group since my first year at UofT. My research includes DeFi protocol design, quantitative finance, market microstructure, payment rails, and digital asset policy ([see more](https://scholar.google.ca/citations?user=XHisYU0AAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao)).

I am currently focused on two related questions:
- **Modernization of Financial Market Infrastructure**<br>
  *In which domains are distributed ledger systems and tokenization truly useful for modernizing FMI?*
- **Market Microstructure for Alternative Assets**<br>
  *How should decentralized markets behave for non-traditional assets like event contracts, compute, perps, everlasting options?*

Outside of school work, I enjoy playing Varsity Lacrosse for UofT, reading, and travelling.
