Options are fundamental risk-management tools in traditional markets, but crypto-options activity remains fragmented across centralized venues and decentralized protocols.

## Scope

The paper surveys market structure, contract design, collateral models, pricing, and the practical barriers that have limited adoption.

## Centralized and decentralized designs

Centralized venues can offer efficient matching and familiar account structures. Decentralized designs offer transparent settlement and composability, but must confront capital efficiency, oracle dependence, and fragmented liquidity.

## A broader role

Deeper options markets could improve more than speculation. They can support structured risk transfer, inform volatility expectations, and help participants manage positions elsewhere in decentralized finance.
