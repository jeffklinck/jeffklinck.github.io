Automated market makers continuously quote liquidity according to a fixed rule. That rule creates a distinctive exposure for liquidity providers when prices move.

## Reframing the exposure

The paper models loss-versus-rebalancing through continuous-installment options. This representation connects an AMM-specific performance measure to more familiar objects in derivatives theory.

## What the model helps compare

- The timing and shape of losses across price paths.
- How alternative market-making rules redistribute exposure.
- The relationship between fee income and embedded optionality.

The broader aim is to make protocol risk easier to compare using a shared financial vocabulary.
