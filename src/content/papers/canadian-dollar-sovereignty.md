Stablecoins are often treated as a narrow crypto-market product. Their larger significance is monetary: a widely used dollar token can become a convenient unit of account and settlement instrument outside the banking system.

## The question

What happens if Canadians begin to use US-dollar stablecoins for ordinary payments, savings, or machine-to-machine commerce? Even without formal dollarization, the Canadian dollar could lose ground in the digital environments where new payment habits form.

## The argument

The paper recommends treating stablecoin adoption as a scenario-planning problem rather than waiting for it to become a measured crisis. Canada should accelerate domestic digital-payment infrastructure, clarify the regulatory path for credible Canadian-dollar instruments, and preserve interoperability with the banking system.

## Why it matters

Monetary sovereignty is not only a question of which notes people hold. It also depends on which unit is easiest to use in the systems where economic activity takes place.
